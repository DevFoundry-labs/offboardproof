# Code of conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep security and privacy central when discussing identity workflows.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact. Enforcement decisions will be handled confidentially and proportionately.

This compact policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
